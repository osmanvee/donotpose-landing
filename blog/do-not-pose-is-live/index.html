DoNotPose is live

I’m creating an interview series called @donotpose ! The inspiration originally was to showcase work of local photographers, in my own signature film style (which I’m still figuring out). There is so much for me to learn from other artists, and I thought it would be a cool way to share them in a dedicated space. I want these mini ‘street documentary’ style portraits to not only capture the work, but also capture insight into the photographer’s mindset. Unscripted and natural moments. What motivates them, how they think about a certain subject, if they follow any protocols or if they just wing it. If you’re a photographer (or any other artist) in the East Coast, (Toronto/Montreal/NYC area) or anywhere else in the world, feel free to reach out, and follow along :)

No polished pitch, no staged moments. Just people, their work, and the city around them. More interviews coming. If you want to be part of it, use the contact form.
