Interview 01

Xavier

Motion and dance photographer

@fx_photographe Montreal, Canada

I met Xavier through the randomest of randoms: on Facebook Marketplace, when I was selling my 4D Nishika camera. I decided to kick off this series with him, mainly because I had never really met many motion photographers, and I was curious. I followed him around and observed his way of taking photos. He is super humble and emphasizes on how much he loves the art of dance. Through photography he stays connected to motion and the dance community.
